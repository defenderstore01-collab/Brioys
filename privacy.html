Privacy Policy

Prototype policy for the Brioys demo store.

This is a store prototype, not a final legal document. Review and replace these terms with your final business policies before going live.

This prototype collects only the information a visitor voluntarily enters into the demo checkout or contact form.

Information Entered

The demo forms may request name, phone number, city, province, delivery address and message details so the prototype can demonstrate an order or support workflow.

How Information Is Used

In the prototype, submitted details are prepared for manual WhatsApp confirmation. There is no connected production database or automated fulfillment system in this demo.

WhatsApp

When a visitor chooses WhatsApp, the browser opens a WhatsApp conversation using the store contact number shown on the site. WhatsApp is a separate service with its own privacy practices.

Cookies & Analytics

This prototype does not intentionally include third-party analytics or advertising trackers in the supplied pages.

Before Launch

A production store should publish a final privacy notice covering the actual hosting, analytics, payment provider, courier and customer-data retention practices.
